# Privacy Policy for AI Keyboard for Android

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 18, 2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how **AI Keyboard for Android** (“the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use our AI-powered Android keyboard application.

The App provides a QWERTY keyboard, an AI chat panel powered by Anthropic’s Claude, and unlimited cloud notes stored through Supabase. The App is offered by an individual developer and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic, Google, Supabase, or any other third-party service.

By creating an account, enabling the keyboard, or using any feature of the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Important Notice About Keyboard and Clipboard Data

Because this App is a keyboard, it may process text that you type while using it. This can include messages, passwords, search terms, notes, personal information, and other content entered in other apps.

**We do not sell your typed text, clipboard content, notes, or AI chat conversations.**

You control what you send to the AI. Text is sent to Claude only when you:

- Tap the **“Send to AI”** button, or
- Submit a prompt in the AI Chat Panel.

Text is committed to another app, such as WhatsApp or a web form, only when you tap the **“Send to App”** button.

We do **not** use your typed text, clipboard content, notes, or AI conversations for advertising, profiling, or selling to data brokers.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information

To use the App, you must create an account. We collect and store:

- Your email address.
- Your account identifier.
- Your authentication method: email/password through Supabase Authentication or Google Sign-In.
- Your account creation and update timestamps.

If you sign in with Google, we receive basic Google account information, such as your name, email address, and Google account identifier, according to the permissions you authorize and Google’s own privacy practices.

### 2.2 Notes

The App lets you create and store unlimited notes in the cloud.

We collect and store:

- The text content of notes you create.
- The date and time a note is created or updated.
- Your account identifier linked to each note.

Notes are stored in Supabase so that they remain available when you sign in from another device.

### 2.3 Clipboard Content

The App includes a clipboard feature that can store up to **100 text items**.

We may collect and store:

- Text that you copy on your device and choose to save in the App.
- The time each clipboard item was saved.
- Your account identifier linked to each clipboard item.

Clipboard items remain available until you delete them, replace them after reaching the 100-item limit, or delete your account.

**Important:** Do not copy sensitive information, such as passwords, banking details, government identification numbers, medical information, or other confidential data, unless you intentionally want to store it in the App.

### 2.4 AI Chat Content

When you use the AI Chat Panel or tap “Send to AI,” we collect and process:

- The prompt or message you submit.
- The response generated by Claude.
- Basic conversation metadata needed to display and manage the chat.

You should not send sensitive personal information to the AI unless you are comfortable with that information being processed by Anthropic.

### 2.5 Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate the App, such as:

- App version.
- Basic error or crash information, if applicable.
- Anonymous usage information needed to maintain and improve the App.

We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, microphone recordings, device identifiers for advertising, or advertising identifiers.
